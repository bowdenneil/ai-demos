📋 Proposal Overview

Waveguide has undertaken a survey of the existing lighting and emergency lighting installations within Google GMQ, Grand Mill Quay, Dublin 4, to assess the feasibility of modernising the building's lighting infrastructure.

💡 General Lighting

€339,140
  • Like-for-like replacement with DALI-compatible LED luminaires
  • Continuous dimming (0–100%)
  • Individual luminaire addressability
  • Daylight-responsive dimming
  • Software configurable
  • Platform for intelligent building controls

🎛️ Lighting Controls

€205,640
  • New Delmatic DALI lighting control system
  • Lighting Control Modules (LCMs) + DALI bus wiring
  • DALI relays for decorative lighting
  • Occupancy-based control + time scheduling
  • Centralised monitoring & BMS integration
  • Aligns with wider Google campus platform

🚨 Emergency Lighting

€166,153
  • Clevertronics HIVE monitored system
  • Wireless RF mesh — no monitoring cabling
  • Automated function & duration testing
  • 12-year battery design life
  • 10-year manufacturer's warranty
  • Independent of DALI control network

Conventional LED vs DALI-Controlled LED

Feature Conventional Fixed-Output LED DALI-Controlled LED
Light Output Fixed Continuous dimming (0–100%)
Energy Demand Constant while energised Matched to operational demand
Task Tuning No Individual luminaire/area adjustment
Daylight Harvesting No Automatic daylight-responsive dimming
Time-Based Control Limited Time scheduling
Configuration Static installation Software configurable
Intelligence Standalone Platform for intelligent building controls
